A project to not just keep the Osborne 1 alive but make it awesome. Modern peripherals that work with the original machine without modifying it, the reverse-engineering that makes that possible — and, downstream, a modern-parts reproduction of the whole computer - the way we all dreamed it could have been.

This is the story around the engineering: the machines, the mods, the people (human and non-human), and the build log.

And yes, this is the Osborne 1 font (extracted from the character ROM and turned into a modern font file). Use the "SCREEN PAC" controls to switch between the original 52 characters wide and the upgrade 80 or 104 character line widths. Just imagine living with a 52x24 screen that was only 5" diagonal!
